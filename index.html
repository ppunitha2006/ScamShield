💡 Simple safety tips

  • Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, or banking password.
  • Do not click unexpected links in messages.
  • Verify requests using your bank's official app or phone number.
  • If unsure, pause and ask someone you trust.

About ScamShield

ScamShield is a simple digital safety project that helps people recognise common signs of online scams.

This prototype checks the message inside your browser. It does not send the text to a server or check links against a live database. Avoid entering passwords, OTPs, or private banking details.
